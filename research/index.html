Research Overview

My research brings together three interconnected areas of work. Across all of them, I am committed to equity, diversity, and justice.

Tertiary education

I study tertiary education policy and practice, including higher education, skills, and lifelong learning. I am interested in the purposes of tertiary education, how it interacts with wider changes in society, and the roles it can play in people's lives and our collective futures.

Example work

  • Changing Higher Education in East Asia
  • How far has the state stepped back: changing governance of higher education in China

Example project

System-Thinking Approaches to Tertiary Education Policymaking in England

Research on Research

My work examines research cultures, assessment, professionals, and wider research and innovation ecosystems. I am interested in how research is organised, valued, governed, and experienced, and how research systems can recognise diverse forms of knowledge and contribution.

Example work

  • Handbook of Meta-Research
  • The impacts of incentives for international publications on research cultures in Chinese humanities and social sciences

Example project

Diverse Contributions to Educational Research

Internationalisation and global higher education

I study internationalisation and the global dynamics of higher education, including how people move across borders, how knowledge circulates, and how institutions connect internationally. My work examines academic mobility, global knowledge systems, and how relationships across countries and regions interact with different knowledge traditions, global inequalities, and changing geopolitical conditions.

Example work

  • Reconceptualising international academic mobility in the global knowledge system
  • Hegemony and inequality in global science: problems of the centre-periphery model

Example project

Transnational Academic Mobility to Global South: An Exploratory Study of International Faculty in China
